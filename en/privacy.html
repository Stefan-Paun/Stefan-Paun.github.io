Privacy. What happens when you visit this site.

As of 16 November 2026 · The German version is the binding one

This site sets no cookies and measures nothing. I do not learn anything about your visit. On the portfolio pages your browser only connects to GitHub: that is where the site is hosted, and where a small live status on the home page comes from.

  • No tracking: no cookies, no statistics, no ads, no forms, no embedded videos, maps or fonts from other providers.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs your IP address for security reasons. I have no access to those logs.
  • Resonanz: your listening data is processed in your browser only. It is not sent to me or to any server of mine. Which other providers your browser asks along the way is listed below.

Who is responsible

I am responsible for this website: Stefan Paun, Vienna, Austria. You can reach me by email at kontakt@stefanpaun.page. This address only forwards to my private mailbox. I use what you write only to reply to you and delete it once the matter is settled.

This notice covers stefanpaun.page with all of its pages and the projects served under this address (Agent-Board demo, Resonanz, Stapelwerk).

Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is delivered by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA; in the EU: GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, the Netherlands). To show the site, your browser has to connect to GitHub's servers.

  • Which data: with every request your browser sends technical details: IP address, time, the address requested, details about browser and device and possibly the page you came from. GitHub states that it logs the IP address of visitors to GitHub Pages sites and stores it for security purposes.
  • What for: to deliver the site and to protect it against attacks and abuse.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in secure and reliable delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • How long: that is up to GitHub. GitHub does not state a fixed period for these logs.
  • Where: GitHub also processes data in the United States and uses service providers, for example a content delivery network (Fastly). GitHub states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and otherwise relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • What I see: nothing. I have no access to these logs and I do not receive any visitor statistics.

More details are in GitHub's privacy statement and in the description of GitHub Pages.

Live status on the home page

The Agent-Board tile shows when the project was last updated. To do that, your browser queries GitHub's public interface (api.github.com) when the home page loads, without a login and without cookies. As with hosting, GitHub sees your IP address and that the request comes from stefanpaun.page. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in showing up-to-date information (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

So that the query does not run again on every page change, your browser keeps the answer for up to ten minutes in session storage (sessionStorage, entry ab-live-v1). It only contains details about the project: date of the last change, version number, number of open issues. Nothing about you is stored there. The entry disappears when you close the tab.

Cookies and browser storage

The portfolio pages (home page, case study, notes) set no cookies. Apart from the entry for the live status, they store nothing in your browser. There are no analytics scripts, no embedded videos, no maps and no fonts from third-party servers.

Projects served under this address

Three of my projects run under stefanpaun.page. They are hosted on GitHub Pages as well, so everything said about hosting above applies to them. None of them sets cookies. Agent-Board and Stapelwerk keep a copy of their own files in your browser from the first visit so that they work without a network. Agent-Board also stores the sound setting when it loads. Agent-Board (live demo)

The demo does not connect to any third-party server. It remembers your settings in your browser's storage (localStorage): language, sound on or off, volume, background music and whether you switched on notifications. It only asks for permission to show notifications when you click the bell.

Stapelwerk

The game does not connect to any third-party server. Your progress, your solutions and your choice of light or dark theme stay in your browser's storage (localStorage).

Resonanz

Resonanz shows listening habits as a night sky. All the calculation happens in your browser. There is no server of mine that listening data is sent to, and I do not see any of it. The sky you see when you open it is a snapshot of my own top lists.

Your browser only connects to other providers when you use the feature in question:

  • Your own sky with a Spotify account: you log in directly with Spotify (accounts.spotify.com). Your browser then fetches your top artists, your top songs and your recently played songs from Spotify (api.spotify.com). For genre and origin it asks MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org) for the names of these artists. That step starts by itself after login, and you can cancel it. MusicBrainz sees your IP address and the names it is asked for.
  • Your own sky from your streaming history: the file you drop in is analysed in your browser only. It is not uploaded anywhere.

The providers are Spotify AB (Sweden) and the MetaBrainz Foundation (USA). What they do with these requests is governed by their own rules: Spotify, MetaBrainz. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in offering the feature you choose (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

In your browser's storage (localStorage) Resonanz keeps: the language, the Spotify client ID you enter, your own sky (when you log in with an account, including the times of your recently played songs), the analysis of your history, the answers from MusicBrainz and, only if you tick the box, a comparison sky. The access token from Spotify is kept in session storage only and disappears when you close the tab. “Back to Stefan’s sky” deletes your sky, the history analysis and the access token.

Resonanz is an independent project and is not affiliated with Spotify or MusicBrainz.

Links to other sites

When you click a link to GitHub or another website, that provider's rules apply there. The provider only learns about your visit once you click.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and the right to object. I do not store any data about visitors to this site myself. For the host's logs, GitHub is the right place to ask. You are still welcome to contact me and I will help you find the right way.

If you think your data is not being processed lawfully here, you can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria that is the Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40-42, 1030 Vienna.

Apart from the connection data that is technically necessary, you do not have to provide anything to use this site. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

If something changes on the site that affects your data, I will update this notice. The date at the top shows which version this is.
